# ADR-001: FRED Series Selection — Narrowing to Semiconductor Supply Chain Indicators

## Intent

Foundation 1 explored six FRED series as candidates across multiple potential
business use cases. When Foundation 2 committed to the Shortage Scout problem
domain, four of those series were eliminated because they measure economy-wide
signals that introduce noise into a semiconductor-specific supply chain model.
This ADR documents why the final four series were chosen and why the others
were rejected.

## Decision

1. Use four FRED series as the exclusive inputs to the pipeline:
   - `IPG3344S` — Industrial Production: Semiconductors (volume signal)
   - `CAPUTLG3344SQ` — Capacity Utilization: Semiconductors (stress signal)
   - `A34STI` — Manufacturer Inventories: Computers & Electronics (buffer signal)
   - `PCU334413334413` — PPI: Semiconductors (upstream cost signal)
2. Retire `CPIAUCSL`, `FEDFUNDS`, `UMCSENT`, and `DSPIC96` as out-of-scope for
   the semiconductor supply chain domain.
3. Replace `PCU334111334111` (Computer Manufacturing PPI) with
   `PCU334413334413` (Semiconductor PPI) for tighter domain granularity.

## Consequences

### Positive

- All four series directly measure semiconductor supply chain health, reducing
  noise in the S_score calculation.
- Tighter domain focus makes the OVERBUY/HOLD recommendation more defensible
  to stakeholders — every input is directly traceable to the supply chain.
- Replacing the broad Computer PPI with the Semiconductor-specific PPI aligns
  the cost signal precisely with the production and capacity signals.

### Negative

- Macro signals (`FEDFUNDS`, `CPIAUCSL`) that could act as leading indicators
  of demand-side pressure are excluded; the model is supply-side only.
- If semiconductor production decouples from broader electronics manufacturing,
  `A34STI` (Computers & Electronics inventories) may become a weaker proxy.

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Pros | Cons | Why Not |
|------------|------|------|---------|
| Retain all 6 Foundation 1 series | Broader economic context | `FEDFUNDS`, `UMCSENT`, `DSPIC96` measure macro sentiment, not semiconductor supply — they add noise, not signal | Domain mismatch with Shortage Scout |
| Keep `PCU334111334111` (Computer Mfg PPI) | Already verified in F1 | Covers all computer manufacturing, diluting the semiconductor-specific signal | Replaced by more granular `PCU334413334413` |
| Add demand-side FRED series (e.g., retail electronics sales) | Would capture demand pressure | No approved demand-side series in scope; increases model complexity | Out of scope for v1 |

## Requirements Checklist

1. [x] Pipeline fetches exactly the four approved series and no others
2. [x] `PCU334413334413` used in place of `PCU334111334111`
3. [x] Retired series produce no output artifacts in the pipeline
4. [x] Series selection documented and traceable to the Shortage Scout problem domain
