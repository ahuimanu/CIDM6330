# Data Analytics Competency Assessment

## Introduction and Scope

This assessment evaluates my preparedness and competency in Data Analytics as developed through the Master of Science in Computer Information Systems and Business Analytics curriculum. It synthesizes coursework, applied projects, and supporting artifacts to assess my strengths, weaknesses, and areas for further development. This appraisal also positions Data Analytics as an integrative discipline that connects data management, software systems, and networking and cybersecurity, while supporting my capstone portfolio and prototype project.

---

## What I Know Well: Established Competencies

### Statistical Analysis and Predictive Modeling

My strongest competencies in Data Analytics lie in statistical modeling and predictive analysis, developed primarily through Data Analytics 6308. I am proficient in linear and logistic regression, exploratory data analysis, hypothesis testing, and model evaluation. I am confident in translating business problems into analytical frameworks and interpreting results in a decision-support context.

---

### Programming for Data Analysis (Python and R)

I developed applied proficiency in Python and R for data cleaning, transformation, exploratory analysis, and model implementation. These tools enable me to analyze structured datasets and support data-driven reasoning in professional contexts.

---

### Business Intelligence and Visual Analytics

Through Data Analytics 6312, I gained experience with Power BI, Tableau, and data transformation workflows. My strongest skills include interpreting and critiquing visualizations and aligning analytical outputs to business decision needs.

---

## Where I Am Weak: Developing Competencies

### Advanced Machine Learning Techniques

I have limited hands-on experience with advanced machine learning algorithms, ensemble methods, and large-scale model optimization. My coursework emphasized interpretability and statistical reasoning over performance-driven modeling.

---

### Unstructured Data Analytics

Most analytical work involved structured datasets, resulting in less experience with text, log, and semi-structured data.

---

### Visualization Design from First Principles

While effective at interpreting visualizations, I have less experience designing custom or novel visualizations and advanced BI customizations.

---

## What I Wish I Knew (and What I May Be Missing)

I recognize gaps related to production analytics systems, including model deployment, monitoring, data pipeline reliability, governance, and ethical considerations. I also seek deeper understanding of scalable analytics architectures.

---

## Integration with Other Curricular Areas

Data Analytics integrates directly with Data Management, Software Systems, and Networking and Cybersecurity by transforming managed data into actionable insight, embedding intelligence into systems, and enabling risk detection and monitoring.

---

## Contribution Toward the Capstone

Data Analytics will serve as the analytical foundation of my capstone portfolio and prototype project. My strengths in applied modeling and business interpretation position me to develop a meaningful, integrative artifact.
